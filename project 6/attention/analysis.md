# Analysis

## Layer 8, Head 10

This attention head appears to focus on direct objects: the noun that is the object of a verb pays attention to the verb that governs it.

Example Sentences:
- She opened the [MASK] slowly.
- The students read the [MASK] before the exam.

## Layer 9, Head 6

This attention head appears to focus on prepositions: a preposition pays attention to the noun that is its object.

Example Sentences:
- The cat slept on the [MASK].
- We walked to the [MASK] after lunch.
